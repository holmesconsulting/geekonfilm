<p><a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2358709/">Nathan Fielder</a> and <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4565262/">Lance Oppenheim</a>'s <a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt45368240/">You Can See Everything</a> follows <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm7266010/">Elizabeth Holmes</a> in the 34 days before the Theranos founder reports to federal prison, after she invites a skeptical film crew to document her every move. Robbie waited until he'd seen it twice, first with his A24 ticket at AMC and again with Amy, and had worked through just about every podcast he could find on it before weighing in.</p>

<p>The verdict: entertaining, confounding, and truly something different. Robbie calls it a magic trick, astounding the first time and still a really good movie the second. The episode opens spoiler-free, covering the bare-bones three-camera shoot, the remarkable use of natural light, and the small domestic moments that end up saying a lot, then gives a clear countdown before getting into spoilers. He also gets into the chaos it's about to cause for awards bodies, and why he's never seen so much conversation in a theater lobby after a movie. Amy's take: original, confusing, and full of mixed feelings.</p>
