<p>Zach Cregger's <a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt35538033/">Resident Evil</a> ditches decades of live-action baggage for a clean, self-contained story: a courier named Bryan, played by <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm3641002/">Austin Abrams</a>, caught in an outbreak he's nowhere near equipped to survive. <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1199107/">Cregger</a> leans into practical effects and a lean, relentless pace, with <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm3236159/">Paul Walter Hauser</a> stealing his scenes in a supporting turn.</p>

<p>The Instant Reaction that went properly viral on TikTok, so much engagement with you all. Reception has been largely enthusiastic across both the game-fan and Cregger-fan camps, though not unanimous, and the $60 million opening weekend backs up the read: a rare video game adaptation that works as a movie in its own right.</p>
