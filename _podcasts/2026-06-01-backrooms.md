  <p>
    My wife is tired of hearing about <a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt26657236/">Backrooms</a>. That should tell you everything. Four stars and a heart. I was 100% bought in from the moment I sat down.
  </p>
  <p>
    <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm10735410/">Kane Parsons</a> signed a contract at 18, shot this film at 19, released it at 20, and just posted the largest opening weekend in A24 history. <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0252230/">Chiwetel Ejiofor</a> and <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4561559/">Renate Reinsve</a> are great — but the real star is the dread. Fluorescent hum, endless yellow wallpaper, footsteps you can't source. The quiet is the horror.
  </p>
  <p>
    See it in a theater, with a crowd. Feel what it's like to be in that space.
