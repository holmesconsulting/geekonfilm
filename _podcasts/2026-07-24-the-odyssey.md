<p>Christopher Nolan's <a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt33764258/">The Odyssey</a> is the first commercial film ever shot entirely on IMAX, and it earns every frame of that scale. <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000354/">Matt Damon</a> carries this movie on his back, leading a stacked ensemble through Nolan's near-perfect vision of Homer's epic.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1500155/">Robert Pattinson</a> quietly gives the best performance in the film as the suitor Antinous, <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000491/">John Leguizamo</a> is devastating as the loyal, half-blind Eumaeus, and <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0680983/">Elliot Page</a>'s Sinon, <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2797744/">Himesh Patel</a>'s Eurylochus, <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0608090/">Samantha Morton</a>'s Circe, and <a href="https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000234/">Charlize Theron</a>'s Calypso all land. Nolan tells the Sack of Troy three separate times across the film, each retelling peeling back more of the cost of how they actually won.</p>

<p>Robbie caught the 7:45am 70mm screening at Harkins Arizona Mills with his wife Amy. One of the most well-paced and intense films he's seen, period.</p>
